StepWay Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StepWay ("StepWay," "we," "us," or "our") accesses, collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information when you use the StepWay Android application.

1. Health and fitness data we access

With your explicit permission, StepWay reads step-count data from Android Health Connect. This can include step totals recorded by your phone, a connected watch, or another fitness application that writes steps to Health Connect. StepWay may read up to the previous 30 days of step data and may access step data in the background so your progress remains current.

StepWay requests read-only access to steps. It does not write to Health Connect and does not request access to heart rate, location, medical records, sleep, nutrition, or other Health Connect data types.

2. How we use health data

StepWay uses step data only to provide user-facing fitness and wellness features: displaying daily and hourly progress, identifying personal weekday and time-of-day activity patterns, calculating adaptive walking goals and quests, and showing progress insights. StepWay does not use Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, data brokerage, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Collection and storage of health data

Imported step totals, hourly step summaries, source labels, and calculated activity patterns are stored locally on your device in StepWay's private app storage. StepWay does not require an account and does not upload Health Connect data or locally recorded step history to us or to the sponsored-content provider. We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise transfer health data to third parties.

4. Phone activity sensor

If you choose phone-based tracking, StepWay uses Android's physical-activity permission and the device step-counter sensor to calculate your step totals. These totals are also processed and stored locally on your device for the same fitness features described above.

5. Sponsored-content data

When StepWay requests or opens the sponsored banner, the banner provider receives standard network information such as your IP address and user-agent information, together with a randomly generated persistent creative identifier, the request date, campaign parameters, and whether the banner was requested or opened. This information is used to deliver, measure, and attribute sponsored content. StepWay does not send your step counts, Health Connect data, activity patterns, Health Connect source applications, or other health information to the banner provider.

6. Data sharing

We do not share health or fitness data. The limited sponsored-content information described in Section 5 is transmitted to our banner provider when the banner is requested or opened. The destination website you choose to open may apply its own privacy policy and terms.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You can decline Health Connect access and use phone-sensor tracking instead.
  • You can revoke StepWay's Health Connect permissions at any time through StepWay's Profile screen or Android Health Connect settings.
  • You can stop phone-sensor tracking by revoking the Physical activity permission in Android settings.
  • You can delete StepWay's locally stored data by using the fresh-profile/reset control in the app or by clearing StepWay's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Revoking Health Connect access stops future access but does not automatically delete summaries StepWay previously imported. Use the deletion options above to remove those local summaries.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored activity information remains on your device until you reset the profile, clear the app's storage, or uninstall StepWay. StepWay has no user account and no health-data server from which separate deletion must be requested. Health Connect retains and deletes its own copy according to your Health Connect settings and the policies of the applications that originally recorded the data.

9. Security

StepWay keeps health information in Android's private app storage and relies on Android platform protections to restrict access. Network communications used to obtain sponsored content use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit access and collection to what is needed for the app's disclosed features.

10. Children

StepWay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Health disclaimer

StepWay is a general fitness and wellness application. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when StepWay's features or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy at the same public URL and change the effective date above.
